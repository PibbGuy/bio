## Deadlock
Genre: Third Person Shooter (TPS), MOBA, Hero Shooter

Developer: Valve

Price: <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Free</span>

Personal Comment: Deadlock is quite possibly, the best MOBA I've ever played AND the best Hero Shooter I've ever played, and that's saying something because I don't typically enjoy Hero Shooters. The game is currently in early alpha and can only be played if you're invited by another playtester, but that's not a problem due to the large amount of people happy to give out invites!

Link: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1422450/Deadlock/

Full Review: [[deadlockreview|Here]]

P.S, if you'd like an invite, go to R/DeadlockTheGame on Reddit and go to the invite megathread, people will happily send you an invite if you ask.

## Library of Ruina
Genre: Turn Based Strategy, Deckbuilder, Visual Novel

Developer: Project Moon

Price: <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">$29.99</span>

Personal Comment: Library of Ruina is a Turn Based Strategy game created by Korean indie studio, Project Moon. The gameplay is simple but deep, the music is just simply *mwah* beautiful, and the story is, to put it plainly,

![[CINEMA.png]]

The story in a visual novel style and is fully voiced. (in korean only but you'll get used to it, the va's are incredible.) The difficulty is probably one of the main reasons to either pick up or put down the game, that being due to the difficulty spike being pretty much vertical with no hand holding. I highly recommend you check this one out if you love a good story and a great challenge with it.

Link: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1256670/Library_Of_Ruina/
