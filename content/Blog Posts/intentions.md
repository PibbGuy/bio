When I was making this website, I fully intended to just use it as a bio site. I was just going to write some things about myself, maybe do a couple extra pages, but that quickly changed when I learned how much stuff you can do in <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">Obsidian</span>, and how fun it would be to blog about stuff as well.

I get bored a lot, typically feeling like I have nothing to do despite having so much I *could* be doing, so to counteract that, I plan to write at least 1 blog a day to keep my mind moving, I've yet to see if this works yet but I guess I'll see in due time.

These'll either be short or long depending on how much effort I decide to put into them, or how much I actually care about the topic. That's basically all though, just wanted to write this for clarity.

See y'all next blog!
