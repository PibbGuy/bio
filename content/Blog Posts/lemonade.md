In case it wasn't obvious, this blog is about a single thing in particular.
## <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">LEMONADE!</span>
I tend to cycle what drinks I consistently consume, and recently I've had quite an infatuation with <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">lemonade</span>! Specifically, I've been buying 12 packs of Minute Maid <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> cans due to how easy they are to store. The pro's of having a mini fridge in your room, I guess.

<span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> is one of those drinks that can be a huge mixed bag depending on it's source, as some companies or people simply can't get it right, while others can perfectly hit the mark. <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> needs to be slightly sour, slightly sweet, and feel refreshing to drink while not being overpowering, and from my tastes, Minute Maid cans simply hit that mark right where I want it. Now don't get me wrong, I love all kinds of <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonades</span>, especially more natural ones. Hell, I'd even drink straight <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemon</span> juice if I felt like it that day, but sometimes you don't care about how healthy the drink is or how much (probably fake) sugar is entering your body, you just want a nice sip of <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> because you're thirsty.

On average there's about 150 calories and 40g of sugar in a can of Minute Maid <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> *(the entire can is 80% sugar)*, and I typically drink about 2 cans a day. That means that I am consistently consuming 300 Calories and 80g of sugar per day off of <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> alone.

Now, I know what you're probably saying.

<span style="color:rgb(146, 208, 80)"><i>"Oh my god! Pibb! You're gonna get fat drinking all that Lemonade! You should think about your beverage habits and drink healthier things!"</i> <br></span>
Well to that I say, suck it. A good perk of having a fast metabolism is the ability to eat/drink basically whatever I want with no repercussions. I cannot speak for myself in the future, however. As of the time of writing this I am only 16, and therefore cannot predict if my metabolism will stay this fast when I am older, but as of now it's, dare I say, fast af.

To conclude, <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Lemonade</span> is good, drink more of it if you want more joy in your life.

*(this blog is not a sponsor nor an ad for Minute Maid Lemonade, I simply like the product.)*
